# GearWatch V2.8 — Company Transmission 2-Pass

V2.8 separates company-level transmission into two explicit stages.

## Pass 1 — Structural

Measures whether the equity is a clean economic expression of the mechanism before looking at current financial statements:

- direct vs second-order exposure
- structural fit
- bottleneck control
- margin capture

Pass 1 can stand alone when financial data is unavailable.

## Pass 2 — Financial

Calibrates Pass 1 with observed company fundamentals:

- operating leverage
- FCF conversion
- balance-sheet resilience
- CFO-to-capex capacity
- size convexity

The financial pass is coverage-weighted. Missing financial dimensions use mechanism priors for diagnostics, but their absence reduces the weight of Pass 2 instead of pretending that the priors are observed company facts.

## Final Transmission

`Final TX = Pass 1 × (1 - effective financial weight) + Pass 2 × effective financial weight`

The effective financial weight is capped at 40% and scales with observed financial coverage. With zero observed financial coverage, Final TX equals Pass 1.

Market momentum remains outside Company Transmission and only enters later in the investment ranking.

## Trading 212

The five seed candidates per mechanism are treated as a user-curated Trading 212 universe. Runtime no longer spends requests on public catalogue pages and the dashboard does not expose broker links.

## UI/UX audit changes

- decision hierarchy is now Causal → Pass 1 → Pass 2 → Final TX → Market
- dense per-company diagnostics use progressive disclosure
- Top 5 table shows the two passes and the final delta directly
- Trading 212 is a simple availability checkmark
- mobile layout collapses the pipeline and company pass strip cleanly
- keyboard activation, Escape-to-close, focus states and reduced-motion support were added
