# GearWatch V2.7 — Company Transmission Engine

Company Transmission is intentionally separate from causal activation and market confirmation.

## Three orthogonal questions

1. **Causal Score** — Is the mechanism actually arming/active?
2. **Transmission Score** — If the mechanism occurs, how cleanly can the shock reach this company's operating economics and free cash flow?
3. **Market Score** — Is the security already confirming or crowding the thesis?

A strong stock chart cannot substitute for a weak mechanism or weak economic pass-through.

## Transmission components

The deterministic score is built from eight components:

- 30% structural exposure / directness
- 15% control of the bottleneck
- 15% margin capture
- 12% operating leverage
- 10% free-cash-flow conversion
- 8% balance-sheet resilience
- 5% capacity to finance/execute expansion
- 5% size convexity

The score intentionally excludes current share-price momentum. Market confirmation enters later in the investment rank.

## Fundamental data

For US/SEC-mapped securities GearWatch refreshes standardized SEC Company Facts and caches them for seven days. It extracts revenue, operating income, cash from operations, capex, assets, liabilities, cash, debt and shares outstanding where available.

If standardized fundamentals are missing — especially for non-US listings — GearWatch uses mechanism-specific structural priors and reduces confidence. It must not invent segment revenue, hedges or company-specific financial facts.

## Economic path

Every mechanism defines:

`shock → operating channel → EBITDA/FCF bridge → known diluters`

Examples include refinery crack-spread capture, tanker TCE/day leverage, missile/energetics bottleneck backlog, AI time-to-power equipment backlog, nuclear-fuel sovereignty and robotics actuator scale.

## Final ranking

For companies that pass the Trading 212 public-catalogue gate:

- 55% Transmission risk-adjusted score
- 30% security-level Market Score
- 15% original structural fit

The mechanism-level Opportunity Score remains separate and combines causal state, mechanism market vote and average transmission quality.

## Trading 212

No API credentials are required. GearWatch checks public Trading 212 instrument pages, caches successful checks and uses them as a catalogue gate. This proves public catalogue presence, not personal-account eligibility or suitability.

## OpenAI jury

The OpenAI jury is bounded and non-authoritative. It audits the already deterministic mapping using supplied evidence, economic path, transmission components and fundamentals. It cannot activate a mechanism, browse, place orders or invent missing company facts.
